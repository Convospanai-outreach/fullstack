### What is CraftMyFunnel and how does it work?
CraftMyFunnel is a governed B2B outreach and qualified meeting workflow platform. It combines intent signal ingestion (LinkedIn Sales Navigator, CSV lists, inbound landing agents), multi-model AI personalization, and a mandatory human-in-the-loop approval queue to generate high-converting, compliant outbound communications without risking brand reputation.

### How does CraftMyFunnel differ from legacy cold email tools like Instantly or Lemlist?
Unlike traditional mass-blasting tools that automate unreviewed messages at high volumes, CraftMyFunnel enforces a governed approval workflow. Every AI-generated draft is placed into an interactive review queue for human sign-off before dispatch, ensuring personalization accuracy and strict deliverability protection.

### How does the Human-in-the-Loop approval queue protect domain reputation?
Every outbound message generated by AI requires explicit review and approval by a team member. This prevents inaccurate AI hallucinations, ensures contextual relevance for each prospect, and maintains high domain sender reputation by keeping bounce and complaint rates exceptionally low.

### Which email providers and channels are supported?
CraftMyFunnel connects directly with Google Workspace and Gmail accounts via OAuth with lease-locked, OIDC-verified synchronization. Custom SMTP/IMAP servers are also supported. Multi-channel touchpoints including LinkedIn and WhatsApp follow in phased releases.

### How does CraftMyFunnel handle data security and multi-tenancy?
CraftMyFunnel implements enterprise-grade multi-tenant data isolation. Sensitive prospect fields utilize deterministic HMAC-SHA256 blind indexing for encrypted query performance, and all operations run through an atomic transactional outbox relay loop with zero data leakage across workspaces.

### What are Vertical Outbound Playbooks?
Vertical Playbooks are pre-engineered sequence blueprints and prompt libraries tailored for specific industries—including SaaS, IT consulting, marketing agencies, staffing, and enterprise software. They streamline campaign creation by embedding proven messaging frameworks and cadence schedules.

### How does the credit and budgeting system work?
Workspaces receive monthly credit quotas that allocate usage across lead enrichment, AI personalization, and sequence execution. Workspace admins can enforce per-user monthly credit caps and hard budget limits to prevent accidental overages.
