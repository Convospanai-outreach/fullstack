# Breach notice templates

Starting points for the customer to edit. Nothing here is sent automatically by CraftMyFunnel. Every template is **[LAWYER REVIEW]** before use. Replace each `[EDIT ME: ...]`.

## 1. Alert from CraftMyFunnel to the customer (email)

Subject: Important: a personal data incident affecting your account

Hello [EDIT ME: customer contact name],

We found a personal data incident on [EDIT ME: date] that affects data held in your CraftMyFunnel account.

What happened: [EDIT ME: plain description]
Data involved: [EDIT ME: for example names, email addresses, phone numbers]
Period: [EDIT ME: from, to]
What we have done: [EDIT ME: how it was stopped and what has been secured]
What we recommend you do: tell the people affected without delay, and prepare your report to the Data Protection Board.

We have attached the list of affected contacts (shared securely). We will send updates as we learn more.

You can reach us at [EDIT ME: contact name and email].

## 3. Notice from the customer to an affected person (WhatsApp)

WhatsApp only allows free text to people who have messaged you in the last 24 hours. For anyone else the message must go out as a template that WhatsApp has approved. Get a template approved ahead of time if you may need one.

Text:

Hello [EDIT ME: first name], this is [EDIT ME: company]. We are telling you about an incident that involved some of your personal data: [EDIT ME: one-line description and data involved]. We have [EDIT ME: what was done]. Questions: [EDIT ME: contact].
