# LinkedIn outreach learnings and product changes

Reviewed 20 November 2026 for CraftMyFunnel's existing LinkedIn outreach product.

The useful pattern across these sources is to choose a specific buyer, bring relevant evidence, offer something concrete and earn a conversation before asking for a meeting. AI can prepare the work; people retain judgment and approval.

## Research scope

Each of the 30 requested names was researched individually. Most direct LinkedIn profile requests returned 999 or 429 responses. The findings below use readable public profiles, indexed first-party posts and the authors' own websites; this is not a review of complete authenticated feeds. Rows marked **indexed** rely on public search-index content because the direct page could not be retrieved. Relative post ages varied between cached and fetched pages, so they are not treated as publication dates.

The supplied follower counts were not used as evidence of effectiveness. Creator-reported revenue, reply rates and meeting counts were not independently validated and are not product benchmarks. Lessons are paraphrased; the application column is our recommendation, not an endorsement by the named person. Sponsored tool posts illustrate a workflow, not proof that a tool or tactic works for CraftMyFunnel.

## Changes applied in this repository

- `apps/api/src/lib/ai/linkedinDraftPrompt.ts`: replaced the two one-line prompts with separate connection and follow-up guidance. Both require grounded professional context, plain language, no fabricated familiarity or proof, sparse-context fallbacks and output containing only the draft. Source context is JSON-quoted and explicitly identified as untrusted data; this is a model instruction, not an injection-proof security boundary.
- Connection notes request a maximum of 300 characters, one genuine reason to connect and no sales or meeting ask. Follow-ups request a maximum of 600 characters, one useful supported point and at most one easy question. These are existing product drafting targets, not a claim about universal LinkedIn account limits.
- `apps/api/src/lib/promptRegistry.ts`: advanced `linkedin.connect` and `linkedin.followUp` to version 2, with new fingerprints so provenance distinguishes the new drafts.

The connection prompt is used by `aiService.generateConnectionMessage`, including the authenticated `/ai/execute` action used by the web proxy. The follow-up prompt is used by `handleSequenceAction` for MESSAGE jobs. These call sites were inspected (Tier A); no paid model call or live send was made.

The main sequence service's LinkedIn flow uses the saved step body to create a human task. It does not call either prompt at that point. Existing saved sequences and previously generated messages therefore keep their text. This change does not add automatic sending, reply capture, new metrics or a new product surface.

## Review and experiment guidance

Before using a generated note, check the target account and buyer role, the source behind its observation, whether the proposed value exists, the actual message length and any prior conversation or refusal. A public event can justify research without justifying a pitch.

For a small pilot, keep the segment, offer and operator constant; change one message angle at a time. Record the cohort size and actual contacts, distinguish positive responses from refusals and automatic replies, and track agreed next steps. LinkedIn reply capture is not currently automatic, so record outcomes manually and do not present a complete reply-rate dashboard. No sample size, reply-rate lift or meeting outcome is promised here.
