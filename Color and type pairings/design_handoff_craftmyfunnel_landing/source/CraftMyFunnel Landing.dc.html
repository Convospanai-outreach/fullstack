How it works

From first signal to booked meeting — every step is governed.

Step 1

Import or capture leads

Every lead enters a shared funnel with full visibility — not a private inbox nobody else can see.

Step 2

Draft, then approve

AI drafts the first message. A manager clears it. It goes out only when approved — no rogue sends.

Step 3

Sync full context

Recent posts, role changes, company moves — reach out with real context, not a generic template.

Step 4

Advance, deliberately

Every stage move is a logged decision. When a lead is meeting-ready, the whole team sees it at once.
